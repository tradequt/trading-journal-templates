# TradeQut trading journal templates

Free templates from [TradeQut](https://tradequt.com), an online trading journal.

TradeQut is an online trading journal for stock, F&O, forex and crypto traders. Instead of typing trades, you upload the file your platform already gives you (MetaTrader 4/5, Zerodha, Dhan, Upstox, Angel One, Groww or any CSV) or a screenshot of the trade list, review the rows and import. MetaTrader commission and swap go into each trade's fees; for Indian brokers it estimates brokerage, STT and the other charges on every trade, so P&L is net. Analytics shows results by pair or strategy, the session you tag, weekday, hour, call or put and expiry day, and what each mistake and broken rule cost. AI reports and chat describe your own trades and never recommend one. 14-day free trial with every feature, then $4.99 or ₹499 a month.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `import-template.csv` | CSV layout for a broker TradeQut does not list ("Other CSV" in the import screen). Columns: Symbol, Side, Quantity, Entry Price, Exit Price, Open Time, Close Time, Stop Loss, Take Profit, Fees, Trade ID. The sample rows are made up. |
| `tradequt-fo-trading-journal.xlsx` | NSE futures and options journal workbook. Type each trade once; quantity, turnover, estimated charges, net P&L and weekday are calculated by formulas, and a summary sheet shows the results by setup and by weekday. |
| `tradequt-fo-trading-journal.csv` | The same 24 columns and five sample rows as plain values, for tools that read CSV. |

How to use the F&O workbook: [tradequt.com/blog/trading-journal-excel-template](https://tradequt.com/blog/trading-journal-excel-template)
